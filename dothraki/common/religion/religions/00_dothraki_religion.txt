### Brief: dothraki_religion
#
# The Faith of the Great Stallion — the Dothraki worship a chief horse-god
# whose herd is the world and whose dead ride the night sky. Structurally
# modeled on Tengrism (nomadic sky-worship, no head, male-dominated) with
# Dothraki-specific deviations:
#   - Female-only clergy (the dosh khaleen at Vaes Dothrak)
#   - Cremation funerals
#   - Fundamentalist pluralism — those who walk are lesser
#   - Witchcraft crime (fear of maegi)
#
